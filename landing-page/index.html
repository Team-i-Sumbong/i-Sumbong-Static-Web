Why i-Sumbong?

Manual complaint handling often slows down community action.

Traditional reporting can be difficult for residents and time-consuming for officials. i-Sumbong addresses common gaps in communication, tracking, and response management.

Scattered Records

Without a central system, monitoring recurring issues and keeping clean documentation becomes harder.

How It Works

A simple process from report submission to resolution.

i-Sumbong keeps the reporting journey clear for both residents and the barangay office.

1 post_add

Submit a Report

Residents enter the concern, add supporting details, and provide the information needed for review.

2 assignment_ind

Review and Assign

Barangay staff assess the report, validate the concern, and coordinate the next action.

3 task_alt

Track and Resolve

Progress becomes easier to monitor until the concern is resolved or properly updated in the system.

About

A digital bridge between residents and barangay officials.

i-Sumbong is built to improve communication, accountability, and transparency in how community concerns are reported and managed.

Why the project matters

The project is designed to improve how community concerns are reported, monitored, and responded to. By moving the reporting process into a more organized digital space, i-Sumbong promotes transparency, accountability, and faster communication within the barangay.

Our goal is to make issue reporting more accessible for residents while helping local officials manage concerns with clearer records and a more consistent workflow.
