Terms of Use for the i-Sumbong web app.

These terms explain the expected use of the i-Sumbong platform, the responsibilities of users, and the limitations of this static prototype version.

Last updated: April 2, 2026

1. Purpose of the platform

i-Sumbong is a barangay issue reporting platform designed to help residents and barangay personnel communicate community concerns more clearly. In this project stage, the website is presented as a static academic prototype and is intended for demonstration and interface review purposes.

2. Acceptable use

Users are expected to use the platform responsibly and in good faith. You agree not to:

  • Submit false, misleading, or harmful reports.
  • Impersonate another resident, official, or organization.
  • Use the platform to harass, threaten, or defame any person or group.
  • Attempt to disrupt, misuse, or abuse the intended academic purpose of the web app.

3. User-provided information

If a user enters names, contact details, report descriptions, or other information into forms, they are responsible for ensuring that the information is accurate and appropriate. Because this version is static, submitted data is not treated as a live official complaint record.

4. Prototype limitation

This version of i-Sumbong does not provide full production functionality such as real account creation, server-side storage, automated verification, or live complaint processing. Links, forms, and flows may be displayed for presentation purposes only.

7. Changes to these terms
