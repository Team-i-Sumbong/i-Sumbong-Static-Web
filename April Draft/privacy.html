Privacy Policy

How i-Sumbong handles user information in this project build.

This privacy policy explains what information may appear in the interface, how it is intended to be used, and the limitations of this static version of the application.

Last updated: April 2, 2026

1. Information that may be requested

The interface may request information such as your name, email address, mobile number, barangay, location details, and the content of a submitted report. These fields are presented to demonstrate how a digital complaint workflow could be structured.

2. How the information is intended to be used

In a complete version of the platform, this information would be used to identify the reporting user, route concerns to the proper barangay personnel, and support status updates for submitted complaints. In this static prototype, the information shown in forms is primarily for demonstration and design review.

3. Storage and processing limitation

This build does not currently provide live server-side processing or full account creation. As a result, information typed into the prototype may not be stored as part of a real operational database or official complaint management system.

4. Data protection principle

i-Sumbong is designed with the intention of supporting responsible handling of user information. Future production versions should implement appropriate safeguards such as secure authentication, access control, protected storage, and administrative accountability.

5. Sharing of information

Information related to a complaint should only be shared with authorized barangay personnel or with parties necessary for report handling, subject to applicable privacy and governance rules. This prototype does not include live sharing features.

6. User responsibility

Users should avoid entering highly sensitive or unnecessary personal information into a prototype interface. Only information relevant to the academic demonstration or intended complaint flow should be provided.

7. Policy updates

This privacy policy may be revised as the project matures from a static prototype into a fuller application. The latest published version in this folder should be treated as the current policy for the active build.

8. Contact

For questions about privacy practices related to the i-Sumbong project, please refer to the contact section of the homepage or use the project contact information provided there.
